A one-image summary of the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**,
drawn as a newspaper broadsheet: leaked data puts Russia's war dead near
500,000, NATO vows space defence over a Russian satellite, the GTA VI
hacker is sentenced in London and walks free, South Korea scrambles jets
over Russian warplanes, and Denmark moves to ban school phones — plus an
"also today" strip.

`entry.png` is the picture. `main.ts` copies it to the grader's output
directory. The poster itself is drawn by `draw.py` from the story data in
`day24.py`, written from that day's wire reports.
